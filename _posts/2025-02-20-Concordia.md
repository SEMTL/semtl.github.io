A meeting of the SEMTL community will be held on **Thursday, Feb 20th, 2025**, at **09:30 to 12:30**. It will take place in the **ER Building (2155 Guy St), room 1072, Concordia University**. Coffee and pastries will be available during the meeting.

### Registration

Please RSVP using [this form](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1fG5uRQbvPufsGLUAnelnuzTSneUVe6L1RDAD7ZTWeIE/edit?gid=1297457195#gid=1297457195).

### Program

This edition of SEMTL will consist of presentations from new faculty and post-doctoral researchers, to highlight new voices in the community.

* 09:00 - 09:30:
  * Arrival
* 09:30 - 10:00:
  * [\[Cristiano Politowski\]](https://cpoli.live/) - Assistant Professor at Ontario Tech University
* 10:00 - 10:30:
  * [\[William Flageol\]](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/William-Flageol) - Assistant Professor at Université du Québec à Trois-Rivières (UQTR)
* 10:30 - 11:00:
  * Coffee break
* 11:00 - 11:30:
  * [\[Minette Zongo Meyo\]](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zongomeyo/) - Post-Doctoral Researcher at Concordia University
* 11:30 - 12:00:
  * [\[Pierre Jaquet\]](https://jacquetpi.github.io/) - Post-Doctoral Researcher at École de Technologie Supérieure (ÉTS)
* 12:00 - 12:30:
  * [\[Bentley Oakes\]](https://bentleyjoakes.github.io/) - Assistant Professor at Polytechnique Montréal
